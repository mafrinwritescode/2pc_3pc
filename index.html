💀 Why 2PC Fails — And How 3PC Fixes It

Two-Phase Commit has one fatal, unsolvable flaw: blocking under coordinator failure. When all participants are stuck in READY with no coordinator, the system is paralysed indefinitely.

Rule ⑧ — The Blocking Deadlock

Coordinator crashes. All participants are in READY. No one can decide.

❌ 2PC Limitations

  • Blocking if coordinator fails in WAIT
  • READY is a trap — can't commit or abort safely
  • Single point of failure — coordinator = bottleneck
  • Recovery needs coordinator restart

✅ What 3PC Solves

  • Non-blocking — system always makes progress
  • PRE-COMMIT signals "all voted YES" to everyone
  • Any participant in PRE can safely drive to COMMIT
  • Tradeoff: 1 extra message round per commit

🔑 The Key Insight

In 2PC, READY only means "I voted YES." It says nothing about others.
In 3PC, PRE-COMMIT means "The coordinator confirmed all participants voted YES."
That one extra fact breaks the deadlock.

⑧ Tricky Case — all queried processes are in READY:
• Cannot COMMIT → coordinator might have sent GLOBAL-ABORT to someone else
• Cannot ABORT → coordinator might have sent GLOBAL-COMMIT to someone else
• → Must block and wait. We cannot simply abort here!

Why not just abort? Unlike timeout-at-WAIT (Rule ④),
participants have already voted YES and resources are locked.
The coordinator may have made a commit decision before crashing.

The Fix: 3PC adds a PRE-COMMIT phase.
If participants are in PRE-COMMIT when the coordinator dies, they know all voted YES — so they can safely elect a new leader and commit on their own. No blocking. No waiting. System always makes progress.
